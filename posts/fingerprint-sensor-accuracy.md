## Introduction
For my tech blog project, I chose to research how accurate fingerprint sensors are under different everyday conditions. Most people, including myself, use fingerprint recognition on their phones, laptops, and other devices without really thinking about how it works unless it doesn't recognize their finger. I want to learn more about how fingerprint sensors work and why they sometimes have trouble recognizing a fingerprint. For my project, I am going to focus on whether having wet or dirty fingers or placing my finger at a different angle affects how well the sensor works. I also want to understand why certain conditions might cause the sensor to reject a fingerprint even when it belongs to the correct person.


I think this topic is important because so many people rely on fingerprint recognition to keep their personal information safe. People use it to unlock their devices, access apps, and sometimes even approve payments. If a fingerprint sensor does not work correctly, it can become frustrating, especially when someone needs to access their device quickly. I also think it is important to understand how dependable this technology actually is when it comes to security. Even though fingerprint recognition is supposed to make accessing devices easier and safer, there are still situations where it might not work as expected. I want to see how much everyday conditions affect its accuracy and whether some conditions cause more problems than others.


The main question I want to answer in this blog is how accurate fingerprint sensors really are when they are used under different conditions. To test this, I plan to use my laptop's fingerprint sensor and try unlocking it 20 different times. I will test it with my finger clean and dry, wet, dirty, and placed at different angles. I will record which attempts work and which ones don't so I can compare the results. I also want to look into how fingerprint recognition compares to other security methods, such as Face ID. By the end of this project, I hope to have a better understanding of how reliable fingerprint sensors are and whether certain everyday conditions make them less accurate.
